Requiem is out, and the conversation around it settled faster than most Resident
Evil launches. The camera toggle worked, the performance held, and the arguments
moved almost immediately to balance rather than technology.

## The camera question answered itself

Capcom's decision to ship both first-person and third-person, switchable at any
time, removed the argument that dominated the run-up to launch. Players simply
picked one.

## Where the feedback actually went

Inventory pressure. It is the recurring note across launch-week discussion:
Requiem asks you to make hard carrying decisions early, and opinion splits
sharply on whether that is tension or friction.

  <figcaption>Encounter density is high early, and inventory space is not. Screenshot: Resident Evil Requiem — via Steam</figcaption>

## What we do not know yet

Whether Capcom treats the inventory feedback as a bug report or a design
disagreement. Those are very different patches, and the studio has not said which
one it is reading.
