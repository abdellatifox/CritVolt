Most open-world post-launch support adds volume: more map, more collectibles, more
things to clear. Shadows has spent its time on the system that changes behaviour
instead, and it is the more interesting choice.

## Why the seasons matter

Seasons in Shadows are not a filter. They close routes, change how sound carries,
and alter what counts as cover. A stealth approach that worked in summer can be
unavailable in winter, which is unusual for a series that normally keeps its
levels stable.

## The wider pattern

It fits what Ubisoft Quebec has said about the game since launch: the studio is
more interested in what the world does to the player than in how much of it there
is. For a series that has been measured in square kilometres for a decade, that is
a real shift.

## What we do not know

How far this goes. Whether seasonal variation stays a world-layer system or gets
built into mission design directly is the question worth watching, and Ubisoft has
not answered it.
