Most gaming headsets are judged on how they sound in a demo and how they look in a photograph. Neither predicts whether you will still want to wear one after three hours.

## What we test

Microphone recordings in a normal room, not a booth, because that is where you will be talking. Clamp force measured and then re-checked after a long session. Software evaluated on whether the headset works properly without it installed.

## Comfort is the whole thing

Weight matters less than pressure distribution. A 320g headset with a wide, soft band is more comfortable than a 260g one that concentrates its clamp on two points. If you wear glasses, this difference is the entire buying decision.

## Wireless or wired

Wireless is worth it if you get up often. If you do not, wired gives better sound for the money and removes an entire category of problem.

## Our pick

The Drift Wireless is the one to buy if you want to stop thinking about headsets. If you are on a budget, the wired Studio is the better product at less than half the price, and the only thing you give up is the cable.
