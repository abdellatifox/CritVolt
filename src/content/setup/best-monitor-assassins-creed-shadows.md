Shadows leans harder on contrast than almost any open-world game of its
generation. Naoe's entire stealth layer is built on the difference between lit and
unlit, which means a panel that cannot hold a black level is not just less pretty —
it is less playable.

## What matters here

**Black level, before anything else.** OLED is a genuine gameplay advantage in
this game, not just a visual one.

**Resolution over refresh.** Shadows is not a competitive shooter. 1440p at 120Hz
serves it better than 1080p at 240Hz, and 4K serves it better still if you can
drive it.

**Size, within reason.** 27in at 1440p and 32in at 4K sit at roughly the same
pixel density. Going bigger without going higher-resolution just makes the image
softer.

## Who should buy what

If your graphics card can feed 4K, the OLED is the clear pick and the difference
in night scenes is not subtle. If it cannot, buy the 1440p IPS and spend the
savings on the card — an OLED fed 40fps is a worse experience than a good IPS fed
100.

## What to skip

Any panel sold on refresh rate alone. In this game it is the specification that
matters least.
