Horizon has spent two games getting bigger. Horizon 6 gets tighter, and it is a
much better game for it.

## The roads

This is the headline. Japan's mix of mountain pass, coastal route and dense urban
grid gives Playground road types that ask different things of the same car, and
the map is small enough that you meet all of them regularly.

## Handling

The physics have not been rebuilt, but they have been re-tuned for roads that
punish sloppiness. Weight transfer reads more clearly, and the difference between
a good line and a great one is now something you feel rather than read off a
timer.

## Where it has not moved

The festival. Horizon's progression still throws cars, wheelspins and unlocks at
you at a rate that flattens any sense of earning something. It was charming in
Horizon 3. Three games later it mostly gets in the way of a driving game that no
longer needs the distraction.

## Verdict

The best Horizon to drive since the series moved to open world. If Playground
ever trims the festival back to match the confidence of the map, the next one
will be remarkable.
